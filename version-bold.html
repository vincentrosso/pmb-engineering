02 · Approach

Understand what's actually happening — then solve it.

01

Start with the problem

Understand what's actually happening before deciding what the solution should be — questioning assumptions, separating symptoms from causes, and reframing problems that have resisted conventional approaches.

02

Move between details and the system

Get deep into the data, process, or failure mechanism when a problem demands it — and step back to see how requirements, manufacturing, risk, schedule, and people interact.

03

Use evidence to decide

Data, statistics, experiments, engineering analysis, and direct observation to reduce uncertainty — matched to the decision at hand, not complexity for its own sake.

04

The objective is progress

Enough understanding to make sound decisions, solve the problem, and move the work forward. Analysis is a tool, not the goal.
