» OUR SERVICES
  • Technical Problem Solving — Complex failures, difficult root-cause investigations, capability problems, and situations where conventional approaches have stalled.
  • Process & Manufacturing Development — Process development, equipment and tooling, validation and qualification, statistical methods, and manufacturing scale-up.
  • Technical Leadership — Senior engineering leadership for critical projects, accelerated timelines, cross-functional teams, and high-visibility technical challenges.
  • Design for Manufacturability — Bringing manufacturing capability upstream so product requirements and designs are grounded in what can actually be produced robustly.
» OUR APPROACH
  1. Start with the problem. Understand what is actually happening before deciding what the solution should be.
  2. Move between details and the system. Get into the data when a problem demands it; step back to see how requirements, manufacturing, risk, schedule and people interact.
  3. Use evidence to decide. Data, statistics, experiments and direct observation, matched to the decision at hand.
  4. The objective is progress. Enough understanding to make sound decisions and move the work forward.
